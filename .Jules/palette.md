## 2026-10-06 - [Add ARIA labels to icon-only buttons]
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like transport controls and sort options) lack context for screen readers and can be confusing for visual users without tooltips. Adding `aria-label` and `title` attributes makes the application more accessible and user-friendly.
**Action:** Always ensure icon-only interactive elements include descriptive `aria-label` attributes for screen readers and `title` attributes for visual tooltips to provide necessary context.
